# Text source

`hongloumeng-120-chapters.txt` is the **public-domain** 120-chapter edition of *Dream of the Red Chamber* (《红楼梦》).

- Author: Cao Xueqin (曹雪芹, d. ~1763); continuation attributed to Gao E (高鹗, d. ~1815).
- Both authors died more than 70 years ago; the work is in the public domain.
- Source: public web edition (古诗文网), chapter-segmented and verified; 120 chapters, 865,292 characters.
- Redistributed here for research and reproducibility purposes only.
